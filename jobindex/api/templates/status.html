Crawl status

Every source's latest crawl and the most recent runs, live from fetch_runs. Machine-readable: /stats, /sources.

A failed fetch never closes jobs

A job is closed only after a successful crawl of its source no longer lists it. When a crawl fails (timeout, HTTP error, a page missing from a paginated list, a sudden drop in the job count) the run is recorded as failed, the source's failure counter goes up, and its jobs are left exactly as they were. Failed runs below therefore always show “–” for closed.
